SOURCE 1 — STARLINK-POWERED FREE WI-FI NOW AVAILABLE AT TALIBON POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE!
Announcement date: September 16, 2026
Official URL: https://tpc.edu.ph/news/starlink-powered-free-wi-fi-now-available-at-talibon-polytechnic-college

Talibon Polytechnic College announced that free Wi-Fi for students, faculty, and staff is active on campus through a partnership with the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT). The service is powered by Starlink.
Hotspot locations listed in the announcement:
- Near the Covered Court
- College Library

How to connect:
1. Open the device settings and turn on Wi-Fi.
2. Select the network named “DICT freewifi4all”.
3. Accept the Terms and Conditions on the portal page to start browsing.

SOURCE 2 — HEIGHTENED IMPLEMENTATION OF CAMPUS SECURITY AND SAFETY PROTOCOLS
Announcement date: September 16, 2026
Official URL: https://tpc.edu.ph/news/heightened-implementation-of-campus-security-and-safety-protocols

The announcement cites CHED Regional Office VII Memorandum No. 248, Series of 2026, and a CHED Memorandum dated August 18, 2026. TPC announced Memorandum No. 28, Series of 2026, issued September 10, 2026. The heightened protocols took effect on September 14, 2026.

Rules listed in the announcement:
1. Controlled vehicle entry: Gate 2 and Gate 3 are open for student vehicles from 6:30 AM to 8:30 AM only, subject to inspection.
2. Re-entry restriction: Student vehicles that exit campus may not re-enter through Gate 2 or Gate 3 without express authorization.
3. Student pedestrian entry: Students must enter through Gate 1 (Main Gate) or Gate 3 (back of the New Building near the NSTP Office) and comply with inspection procedures.
4. Gate 3 access hours: Gate 3 is open for student access until 7:00 PM. After 7:00 PM, students must use Gate 1 for entry and exit.
5. Mandatory security inspection: Students, visitors, and personnel are subject to screening of bags, packages, and personal belongings.
6. Weapons prohibition: Firearms, knives, bladed weapons, and other dangerous materials are prohibited on campus.
7. Compliance: Everyone is expected to cooperate with security personnel. Refusal or violations may be investigated and addressed under college policy.
